# PRODUCT.md — CareerPilot

## Product truth
AI-assisted job search companion: resume upload and parsing, job import and analysis,
transparent compatibility scoring (skill overlap only, never hiring probability),
interview question generation, application tracker. Demo mode with no sign-up.
Auth: Supabase. Routes: `/`, `/login`, `/register`, `/forgot-password`,
`/dashboard`, `/resumes`, `/resumes/:id`, `/jobs`, `/jobs/:id`, `/match`,
`/interview`, `/applications`, `/demo`, `/settings`.
Landing anchors (stable, tracked): `#features`, `#how-it-works`, `#faq`.
Primary CTA labels (stable): "Get started" / "Get started free", "Sign in",
"Open dashboard", "Try the demo".

## Audience
Job seekers who want an honest journal, not a black-box predictor.
